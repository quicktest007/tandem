How it works

A simple rhythm for lasting progress.

  1. 01

    Choose what matters

    Create a habit or goal around health, learning, creativity, work, relationships, digital wellness, or personal growth.

  2. 02

    Invite your person

    Build your Tandem with a friend, spouse, coworker, coach, family member, or someone who shares your goal.

  3. 03

    Check in

    Share quick updates, reflect on progress, celebrate wins, and be honest when the week does not go as planned.

  4. 04

    Keep moving

    Receive gentle nudges, encouragement, and reminders that help small actions become lasting routines.
